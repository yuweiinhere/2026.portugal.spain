About check-in and boarding passes

This booking confirmation is not a boarding pass. You will get it once you have checked in online or at the airport.

Online check-in is available from 4 days up to two and a half hours before your flight. If you have purchased a seat, you can download and print your boarding passes as soon as your purchase is complete. After checking in, you can print your boarding pass or download it on your smartphone.

Remember to check-in online and make sure you print out your boarding passes or download them on your smartphone before you arrive at the airport. Otherwise, if you don’t have pre-paid our airport check-in service, the cost of obtaining the boarding passes at the airport will be €30.00 per passenger (please check te fees table).

Online check-in might not be allowed in some cases and passengers will need to collect the boarding pass for free at the airport. Read more information.

Hand luggage policy

All our customers can take a small bag on board, free of charge (max. 40x30x20 cm), that must be fit under the seat in front of you. Customers who have included the Priority Boarding service may carry a second piece of hand luggage on board (max. 55×40×25 cm). Both pieces together should weigh a maximum of 10 kg.
